## Two accent layers

The load-bearing decision. Red measures 1.2° from the fault ink, and there is no
bright red that clears the accent gate's 30°: the band between the fault
and the warning is 34.8° wide. So red is admitted exactly where a colliding
tenant accent is admitted, and barred everywhere a state ramp renders.

| Layer | Where | Resolved by |
|---|---|---|
| Brand — red `#D91F11` | wordmark, livery, badge, S1 marketing | `stateRamp: false` in the surface registry |
| Instrument — arc `#00A3D9` | every surface that shows state | the default |

A surface that forgets to declare renders in the neutral: the unsafe direction
requires an explicit opt-in.

## Type schedule

| Role | Face | Job |
|---|---|---|
| wordmark | Yellowtail | The masthead lockup, and nothing else |
| display | IBM Plex Sans Condensed | Plate titles, buttons, headings |
| label | IBM Plex Sans | Descriptors, eyebrows, the company line — set in caps at +0.14em and up |
| text | IBM Plex Sans | Body copy, prose, contracts |
| instrument | IBM Plex Mono | Every numeral in the company, without exception |

The wordmark is a mark, not a typeface: it appears once per surface and never
below 28px. Every numeral in the company is set in the instrument face with
tabular figures, self-hosted — never from a third-party host, which the guard
enforces.
